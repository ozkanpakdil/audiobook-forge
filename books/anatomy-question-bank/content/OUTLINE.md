# Anatomy — A Narrated Question and Answer Bank — Chapter Briefs

One brief per chapter. The chapter must contain exactly the number of questions
stated here, in the order given, and must follow the contract in PLAN.md. Length,
format, spelling and safety rules live in PLAN.md, not here.

The bank holds 11,600 questions in 116 chapters across the twenty
categories of the source blueprint. Question numbering runs continuously from the
first question of chapter one to the last question of the final chapter, so each
brief states the number the chapter opens on.

1,740 verbal image description, 1,160 relationships, 1,160 clinical anatomy, 580
true or false, 580 matching, 348 short answer, 232 spatial reasoning.

1,740 expert.
